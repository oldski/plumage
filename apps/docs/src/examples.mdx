# Examples

Two full screens built only from Plumage components and patterns, the way a product
would put them together. They exist to show the system working as a whole, not one part
at a time.

- **Finance overview**: an app frame with navigation, a page header, headline figures with
  their changes, value over time against a benchmark, allocation by sector, and a sortable,
  searchable table of holdings.
- **Health day**: the same frame around a single day: progress toward the day's targets,
  macros split by calories, twelve weeks of consistency, a log of the day so far, and a
  quick-entry field in the header.

## One screen, every brand

Neither screen names a brand or branches on one. Everything that makes it look like a
product comes from tokens, so the toolbar's **Brand**, **Mode**, and **Density** switch the
whole screen at once: colors, type, radius, spacing, chart palettes, and control heights.

Set **View** to _All brands and modes_ to see a screen in all four brands, light and dark,
side by side. A finance screen in a health brand is deliberate: it shows the components do
not care which product they are in.

## The data

Every figure is invented: the holdings, the prices, the meals, the step counts. Nothing is
real or tied to a product, and nothing reads the current date, so the screens look the same
every day and the visual regression suite can compare them.
