# 0039. The hummingbird and chickadee brands

- **Status:** Accepted, finalized by [0040](0040-health-brands-finalized-and-health-patterns.md)
- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Scope:** Plumage

## Context

The monorepo adds two health apps, and each app is a Plumage brand. The brief asks for:

- **hummingbird:** iridescent emerald green with a ruby-red secondary accent (the
  ruby-throated hummingbird); energetic but clean; comfortable density.
- **chickadee:** soft charcoal (the black cap), warm buff, and cream; friendly, cozy,
  kitchen-warm; comfortable density.
- Body and numeric fonts are Switzer for both; display fonts are not chosen yet, so
  Switzer stands in.

Finch and Sparrow must render exactly as before, and the contrast checks, Storybook
switchers, and visual regression must cover all four brands. Brand palettes come from
luminance-matched ramps (ADR 0004), so step N of any ramp has the same contrast as step N
of every other; a brand only chooses which ramp fills each role.

## Options considered

For hummingbird's feedback colors, the problem is that emerald sits beside green:

1. **Keep positive on green.** Success badges and up arrows would read as the brand
   color, and the brand color as success.
2. **Move positive to teal, and pull emerald toward blue-green.** Emerald at OKLCH hue 160
   to 164 sits between green (146 to 150) and teal (182 to 188); teal is the farther of
   the two. Chosen. Deltas never rely on color alone (an arrow and text carry the sign),
   so a teal "up" is still unambiguous.

For chickadee's charcoal:

1. **Charcoal as the signature fill (primary buttons).** The accent fill is shared by
   light and dark mode, and a charcoal button disappears on a dark surface.
2. **Charcoal as the dark end of the neutral ramp, buff as the accent.** One new neutral
   ramp, `cream`, runs from buttery cream through to a soft warm charcoal, so light mode
   is cream with charcoal text and dark mode is charcoal with cream text. The signature
   fill is buff, step 300, with charcoal text on it, as Finch's gold takes dark text.
   Chosen.

For hummingbird's ruby:

1. **Add a secondary-accent role now.** Every brand would need values for it, and no
   component uses one yet, so the tokens would be shipped without a consumer or a test.
2. **Add the `ruby` ramp now, and the role in step 13.** Chosen. Step 13 finalizes both
   health brands, and a secondary accent needs a decision about what uses it (charts,
   highlights, the heart rate series) before it becomes a semantic token.

## Decision

Four new primitive ramps, generated by `scripts/ramps.config.ts` (existing ramps came out
byte for byte unchanged):

| Ramp      | Kind      | OKLCH hue  | Chroma         | Use                                    |
| --------- | --------- | ---------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| `emerald` | chromatic | 160 to 164 | peak 0.17      | hummingbird accent                     |
| `ruby`    | chromatic | 10 to 12   | peak 0.22      | reserved: hummingbird secondary accent |
| `buff`    | chromatic | 78 to 62   | peak 0.085     | chickadee accent                       |
| `cream`   | neutral   | 85         | 0.018 to 0.012 | chickadee neutrals                     |

The brands:

| Role     | hummingbird                        | chickadee                                   |
| -------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Neutral  | gray                               | cream                                       |
| Accent   | emerald; fill 600, white text      | buff; fill 300, charcoal (`cream.975`) text |
| Positive | teal                               | green                                       |
| Negative | red                                | red                                         |
| Warning  | gold (orange sits beside ruby)     | orange (gold sits beside buff)              |
| Info     | blue                               | blue                                        |
| Radius   | 50, 100, 200, 250 (Finch-like)     | 100, 200, 300, 400 (the roundest)           |
| Motion   | 100, 150, 250 ms (quick)           | 150, 200, 300 ms (calm, like Sparrow)       |
| Fonts    | Switzer for body, numeric, display | Switzer for body, numeric, display          |
| Density  | comfortable                        | comfortable                                 |

Both are registered in `src/plumage.resolver.json` with their display names and default
density, which is all Storybook's toolbar and _All brands and modes_ view, the Figma Brand
collection, the CSS, and the TypeScript `Brand` type need. The visual regression suite now
runs every story in 4 brands × 2 modes × 2 densities.

## Consequences

- **Good:** all 701 token checks pass for four brands in both modes (text and UI contrast,
  the ramp contract, chart palettes, tier rules), and Finch and Sparrow resolve to
  identical values in all eight of their contexts, checked by building the old and new
  sources and comparing every token.
- **Good:** no component changed; the brands are data.
- **Bad:** the visual baselines double, to about 1,500 screenshots.
- **Follow-ups (step 13):** choose display faces; decide the secondary-accent role and
  where ruby appears; look at both brands on real Hummingbird and Chickadee screens and
  adjust (chickadee's buff fill and hummingbird's teal positive are the two calls most
  worth a second look).
