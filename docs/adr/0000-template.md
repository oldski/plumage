# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by NNNN (link the record)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Scope:** Plumage | Finch | Repo

## Context

What problem are we solving, and what forces are in play (requirements, constraints,
deadlines, team knowledge)? State facts, not the answer.

## Options considered

1. **Option A.** One or two sentences, with the main pro and con.
2. **Option B.** Same.
3. **Option C.** Same.

## Decision

The option we chose, stated plainly, plus the essential details someone needs to
implement or review it.

## Consequences

- **Good:** what gets easier.
- **Bad:** what gets harder, what we accept as a cost.
- **Follow-ups:** work this decision creates, and what would make us revisit it.

## References

Links to specs, spikes, discussions, or code.
