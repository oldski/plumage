Add `ProgressRing`, a round `Progress` for how close something is to a target, in `sm` and
`lg` sizes from the new semantic tokens `size.figure.sm` and `size.figure.lg`
(`size-figure-sm`, `size-figure-lg`), which follow density, and a `4rem` size primitive
(`size.1600`). Add the `Timeline` pattern: dated moments in an ordered list joined by a
line, with done and upcoming markers. Sparrow's goals page and payoff planner use them. Dialogs taller than the
window now scroll inside themselves instead of running off the screen.
