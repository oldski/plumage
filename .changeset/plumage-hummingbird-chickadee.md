Add the **hummingbird** brand (an emerald accent on cool gray, positive roles on teal, small
radii, quick motion, comfortable density) and the **chickadee** brand (a buff accent with
soft charcoal text on cream neutrals, rounder corners, calm motion, comfortable density).

`ruby` (reserved for a secondary accent), `buff`, and `cream`. Finch and Sparrow resolve to
exactly the same values as before.
